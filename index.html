Crowd Line: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Crowd Line ("the game") is made by Nazar Bystren ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on iPhone.

1. The short version

Crowd Line has no accounts, no ads and asks for no personal details. Your progress stays on your iPhone. To learn which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends gameplay statistics (for example "level 8 started / won / lost") to GameAnalytics under a random identifier that is not your name, email or advertising ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it asks you first, and you can play just the same if you say no. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to track you across other apps.

2. What the game stores on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • your progress: unlocked levels, stars, coins, upgrades, daily reward, chests and win streak;
  • your settings: sound on or off, and your answer to the statistics question;
  • simple counters of game events (for example, how many levels were started or won).

This data is kept in the iPhone's standard app storage. Deleting the game deletes it. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include it in your backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

3. What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts or logins, and asks for no name, email or other personal details.
  • It works offline; the only thing it sends, when online, is the gameplay statistics described in section 4.
  • It contains no advertising and no social tools, and never asks for your advertising identifier.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you choose to share crash reports or usage data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple collects that data and passes it to me in anonymous, combined form. Apple's privacy policy covers this: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Children

Crowd Line is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and it knowingly collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

6. Your rights

The only data that leaves your iPhone is the gameplay statistics in section 4, held by GameAnalytics under a random device identifier. You can ask for it to be accessed or deleted by contacting me (section 7) or GameAnalytics. To remove everything the game has saved on your iPhone, delete the app.

7. Changes and contact

If the game changes, for example by adding ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
