Crowd Line: Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to odasskywars64@gmail.com. Please add your iPhone model and the level number if something went wrong.

How to play

Draw a wall with your finger, then press START. The crowd walks around your walls: lead it into saws, pits and fire. Traps get tired and rest for a moment, so keep steering the crowd from trap to trap. Tap a crumbling wall to mend it.

Start over

iPhone Settings → Crowd Line → Reset progress, then open the game again.
